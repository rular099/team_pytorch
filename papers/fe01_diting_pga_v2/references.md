# 参考文献

1. Münchmeyer J, Bindi D, Leser U, Tilmann F. 2021. The transformer earthquake alerting model: a new versatile approach to earthquake early warning. Geophysical Journal International, 225(1), 646–656. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1093/gji/ggaa609)。期刊卷年为 2021，在线提前出版日为 2020-12-24。
2. Zhu W, Beroza G C. 2019. PhaseNet: a deep-neural-network-based seismic arrival-time picking method. Geophysical Journal International, 216(1), 261–273. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1093/gji/ggy423)。架构论文不等于本文的 STEAD v2 权重来源。
3. Mousavi S M, Ellsworth W L, Zhu W, Chuang L Y, Beroza G C. 2020. Earthquake transformer—an attentive deep-learning model for simultaneous earthquake detection and phase picking. Nature Communications, 11, 3952. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-020-17591-w)。Chuang 的全名按原文为 Lindsay Y. Chuang。
4. Woollam J, Münchmeyer J, Tilmann F, Rietbrock A, Lange D, Bornstein T, Diehl T, Giunchi C, Haslinger F, Jozinović D, Michelini A, Saul J, Soto H. 2022. SeisBench—A Toolbox for Machine Learning in Seismology. Seismological Research Letters, 93(3), 1695–1709. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1785/0220210324)。本文锁定版本 0.10.1 来源为归档环境元数据，非当前文档版本。
5. National Research Institute for Earth Science and Disaster Resilience. 2019. NIED K-NET, KiK-net. [数据 DOI](https://doi.org/10.17598/NIED.0004)。本文使用其中 K-NET 数据，数据 DOI 名称中的 KiK-net 不表示本研究同时使用两网。
6. Bishop C M. 1994. Mixture Density Networks. Aston University, NCRG/94/004. [作者报告](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2016/02/bishop-ncrg-94-004.pdf)。机构目录仍保留早期编号 4288，报告首页明确给出 NCRG/94/004；无 DOI，不虚构 DOI。
7. Gneiting T, Raftery A E. 2007. Strictly Proper Scoring Rules, Prediction, and Estimation. Journal of the American Statistical Association, 102(477), 359–378. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1198/016214506000001437)。采用期刊 2007 年，而非网页迁移后的 2012 在线标记。
8. Mohammed M, Saad O M, Keshk A, Ahmed H M. 2025. Predicting peak ground acceleration using the ConvMixer network. Earthquake Science, 38, 126–135. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.eqs.2024.11.005)。按正式作者稿核对作者顺序和页码，早期预印本的顺序不同；不跨论文比较误差。
9. Shin Y, Jeong W. 2026. Conformal prediction for earthquake early warning: finite-sample coverage guarantees for prediction intervals of peak ground acceleration. Geophysical Journal International, accepted manuscript, online 2026-10-05. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1093/gji/ggag415)。发表日期早于本稿制备；仅引用区间校准背景，不引入其其他实验叙事。

核验时间为 2026-11-16。检索成功、索引可见和直接打开失败分别记录于 [references_audit.json](references_audit.json)。未能证实的 DiTing MAE 1200M 精确论文、语料与许可不放入参考文献表，见 GAPS；不以同名数据集或其他模型代替。本文没有复制外部论文图像，图1为依据本项目代码新绘制的示意图。
